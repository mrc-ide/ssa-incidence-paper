# Key-population data changes (18 Nov 2026)

All changes are in the key-population cleaning step of `R/data_functions.R` (`read_additional_data()` and
`align_observations()`). The pipeline was rerun after them:

- `prep/build_modelled_incidence.R`
- `R/01_data.R`
- `R/02_fit_main.R`, `R/03_fit_loo.R`, `R/05_risk_of_bias.R`
- `paper_figures.R`, `paper_numbers.R`

`R/04` (cohorts and PHIA) was not rerun. Its inputs are unchanged, and the committed outputs are kept.

## Changes

1. **FSW data from 1990.** The key-population year filter is now `year >= 1990` (was `year > 1995`). This adds:
   - Fowke (Kenya 1990)
   - McClelland 1993-95 (Kenya)
   - van der Loeff (Guinea-Bissau 1994)
   - Roddy (Cameroon 1995, placebo arm)

   Cameroon area IDs in `kp_incidence.csv` use Naomi's old scheme. They are now mapped as `CMR_1_3` → `CMR_02_11`
   (Douala) and `CMR_1_12` → `CMR_02_12` (Yaoundé).
2. **Multi-site studies are one observation.** Some studies report one pooled rate, which `kp_incidence.csv`
   repeats on a row per site. Each pooled rate now has one `idx` matched to all its sites, with the median modelled
   incidence across sites, as for multi-site review studies. This applies to Roddy, Lyons (FSW and MSM),
   Lippman and Malama. Before, each was counted once per site.
3. **Site-level numbers from the primary papers.**
   - Sandfort 2021 (HPTN 075, Table 2): the South Africa total of 17/147.4 is now Cape Town 10/69.3 and
     Soweto 7/78.1.
   - Sullivan 2020 (Sibanye, Table S1): the age rows in the file were Cape Town's. Port Elizabeth is now
     1/6.9 (18-19), 1/45.4 (20-24) and 1/23.9 (25+).
4. **Lyons FSW** is matched to Dakar, Mbour and Thiès. The extraction left out Mbour.
5. **BED-based estimates dropped**, as for the review studies:
   - Botswana MoH 2012 BBSS (BED-CEIA in a RITA). Its 46 infections were also the BED count before RITA
     correction.
   - Forbi 2011 (BED-CEIA only).

   Nouaman (in-house limiting EIA) and Kassanjee (LAg-Avidity plus viral load) stay. No MSM study used a
   recency assay.

Final data: 176 studies, 3,508 observations, about 20,500 infections. Table 1 now has 29 FSW studies. In Figure 1,
the Jones et al. box shows "Excluded (N=4): data before 1990 (N=2), BED assay (N=2)"; FSW studies N=28.
